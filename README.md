## The image

A lighthouse on a rocky cliff at night, its beam of light frozen solid into a giant golden roller-coaster loop in the sky, a tiny glowing train of light racing around the loop with sparks and stars bursting from the track. One huge golden ring, one thrilling moment — it reads at a glance, even small.

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, byte for byte.
